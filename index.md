Artists like <a href="https://open.spotify.com/artist/1oPRcJUkloHaRLYx0olBLJ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Magdelena Bay</a> and <a href="https://open.spotify.com/artist/5zbFRbEu3FDkqN4j1awLLO" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dev Lemons</a> define your taste for art pop and hyperpop, showcasing a preference for intricate electronic textures and catchy melodies. This sits alongside the raw energy of experimental hip hop from <a href="https://open.spotify.com/artist/6yJ6QQ3Y5l0s0tn7b0arrO" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">JPEGMAFIA</a> and the lyrical depth of <a href="https://open.spotify.com/artist/2YZyLoL8N0Wb9xBt1NhZWg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Kendrick Lamar</a>. A common thread is leaning towards neo-psychedelic influences and artists who push genre boundaries, from indie punk to math rock.</p>
